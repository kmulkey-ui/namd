## Actions that widen eligibility (ranked)

1. **SAM.gov registration (UEI).** Free; takes weeks. Required for federal awards. The scanner flags every federal notice until `sam_registered` is set to `true` in `rfp_scanner/config.json`.
2. **Texas CMBL registration** with the Comptroller, so state agencies send solicitations directly.
3. **Texas HUB certification** if ownership qualifies (51%+ minority, woman or service-disabled veteran). Also makes United CCR a target subcontractor for primes with HUB goals.
4. **TDLR mold remediation contractor license.** Texas mold work is flagged until `TDLR-MOLD-REMEDIATION` is added to `licenses_held`.
5. **Surety bonding letter** with single and aggregate limits.
6. **Cooperative contracts** (TIPS, BuyBoard, Choice Partners). One award lets ISDs, cities and counties buy without their own bid.
7. **IICRC Certified Firm** status for restoration RFPs.
