    To widen eligibility

    1. Register in SAM.gov for a UEI. Free; takes a few weeks; required for any federal award.
    2. Register on the Texas Comptroller's CMBL so state agencies send solicitations directly.
    3. Check Texas HUB eligibility (at least 51% owned by a minority, woman or service-disabled veteran).
    4. Get a TDLR mold remediation contractor license before bidding Texas mold work.
    5. Obtain a surety bonding letter with single and aggregate limits.
    6. Pursue cooperative contracts (TIPS, BuyBoard, Choice Partners) so ISDs, cities and counties can buy without their own bid.
    7. Pursue IICRC Certified Firm status for restoration RFPs.
