To widen eligibility

  1. Register in SAM.gov for a UEI. Free, takes a few weeks, and is required for any federal award, including FEMA-funded local work that checks exclusions.
  2. Register on the Texas Comptroller's CMBL (Centralized Master Bidders List) so state agencies send solicitations directly.
  3. Check HUB eligibility. Texas HUB certification requires at least 51% ownership by a minority, woman or service-disabled veteran. If it applies, it adds weight on state contracts and makes United CCR attractive as a subcontractor to prime bidders with HUB goals.
  4. Get a TDLR mold remediation contractor license before bidding Texas mold work; the scanner flags every Texas mold job until one is on file.
  5. Obtain a bonding letter from a surety with single and aggregate limits. Most public construction above $50,000 to $100,000 needs payment and performance bonds.
  6. Pursue cooperative contracts (TIPS, BuyBoard, Choice Partners). A co-op award lets ISDs, cities and counties buy from United CCR without running their own bid.
  7. Pursue IICRC Certified Firm status; restoration RFPs often require IICRC-certified technicians.
